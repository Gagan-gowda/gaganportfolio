PROJECT OVERVIEW

A cleaner way to navigate SharePoint.

The Expandable Navigation Card is a custom SharePoint Framework (SPFx) web part designed to provide an organized, user-friendly navigation experience in SharePoint Online.

It groups resources into card-based sections that users can expand when needed, making forms, policies, templates and business applications easier to find without overcrowding the page.

THE GOALMake important SharePoint resources easier to discover without cluttering the page.
02

THE PROBLEM

Too many links. Too much clutter.

01

Cluttered navigation

Large portals can contain a significant number of resources spread across different pages and libraries.

02

Difficult discovery

Users can struggle to locate important documents, forms and frequently used resources.

03

Poor organization

Traditional link lists do not clearly separate different business functions.

04

Time wasted searching

People spend unnecessary time looking for resources that should be immediately accessible.

03

THE SOLUTION

Organize. Expand. Navigate.

The solution presents navigation in a clear card-based layout. Each card represents a business area and expands to reveal its related links and resources.

01Categorize

Group related resources into meaningful business categories.

02Expand

Reveal additional navigation only when it is needed.

03Navigate

Give users quick access to the resource they need.

04

KEY FEATURES

Designed around the user experience.

01

Responsive design

Card-based navigation adapts to different screen sizes and layouts.

02

Expandable sections

Users can control the amount of information displayed.

03

Multiple categories

Supports business-focused resource categories.

04

Configurable navigation

Navigation items can be configured without changing code.
